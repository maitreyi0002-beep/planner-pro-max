# Quality bars

Run every check before delivering. Each one exists because skipping it produces a specific, recognisable failure.

---

## The customisation test

**Would swapping in a different person's answers produce nearly the same document?**

If yes, the customisation failed. Go back to the positioning line and start again — everything downstream inherits it, so a generic positioning produces a generic plan no matter how much detail sits underneath.

This is the check that matters most. A plan that could belong to anyone is worth roughly nothing to the person holding it, and they can tell.

---

## Contradiction checks

**Read the plan back against what they refused.** Question 8, and the constraints branch off question 2. Delete anything they said they wouldn't do or legally can't. This is a deletion pass, not a judgement call — if they said no video, every video suggestion goes, however good.

**Check the schedule against stated hours.** At most 80%, with a day off. If it totals more, cut scope rather than compressing blocks.

**Check the takes against the risk appetite.** A plan full of takes they won't publish is a failed plan that looks complete.

**Check platform advice against their actual accounts.** Don't prescribe a posting-first strategy to someone with 200 followers.

---

## Evidence checks

**Every take has a claim, evidence, and a rebuttal.** A claim without evidence is an opinion. A claim without a rebuttal is one they'll lose in public.

**No invented statistics anywhere.** Every number traces to a real source or to their own work. If you couldn't find a number, the take should have been rebuilt around something provable — go back and do that rather than shipping a soft claim.

**Sources are linked, not just named.** They will be asked where something came from.

---

## Structural checks

- At most five pillars
- At most three platforms
- Every gate is checkable by someone other than them
- Kill rules name a day and a threshold
- The project slate says how many to actually pick
- The positioning line appears in the plan verbatim more than once — opening, and wherever they're told to use it

---

## Tone checks

**No coach voice.** Scan for "unlock", "leverage", "game-changer", "in today's landscape", "journey", "10x". Delete and rewrite in concrete nouns.

**No promised outcomes.** The plan is a method. Any sentence implying a result the plan cannot produce comes out. This matters legally and it matters for trust — a plan that promises a job is a plan they'll resent in week nine.

**The honest expectation is present.** Weeks one to four feel like nothing is happening. If the plan doesn't say so, it's setting them up to quit on schedule.

---

## The read-aloud check

Read the positioning line and the first pillar out loud. If you'd be embarrassed to say it to the person's face — too grand, too vague, too obviously generated — rewrite before sending.
